Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 (Structural Draft) • Privacy-Safe Certificate Verification Standards

Draft Notice — Pending Legal Counsel Review

This privacy page outlines intended data categories and protection practices for prototype demonstration. It is not a binding legal privacy notice until finalized by ECDL's legal and information governance officers.

Table of Contents

1. Information Categories Collected 2. Purpose of Processing 3. Corporate Client & HR Reporting 4. Public Certificate Verification Scope 5. Storage & Retention Principles 6. Learner Rights & Contact

01 Information Categories Collected

[Draft Pending Legal Review] During course registration, corporate nomination, or trainer onboarding, Open ECDL platforms collect only operational contact, billing, and attendance records required to administer training cohorts.

  • Identity and professional details (name, designation, organisation, business email, phone number).
  • Learning participation data (session attendance, assessment completion, evaluation feedback).
  • Billing and statutory VAT documentation metadata (e.g., corporate BIN or Mushak 6.3 details where applicable).

02 Purpose of Processing

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Operational data is used to administer cohort seating, distribute courseware, track attendance compliance, compute learning effectiveness metrics, and issue verified training certificates.

03 Corporate Client & HR Reporting

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Where an employee is sponsored and nominated by a corporate employer, attendance status and completion metrics for that sponsored programme may be shared with authorised HR/L&D contacts inside the client's Corporate Portal workspace.

04 Public Certificate Verification Scope

[Draft Pending Legal Review] The public Certificate Verification Portal enforces strict data minimisation. Lookup queries display only masked/partial recipient identifiers, programme title, issue date, and status (Valid or Revoked)—never phone numbers, private addresses, or raw assessment scores.

05 Storage & Retention Principles

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Retention windows for statutory tax invoices, quality audit trails, and credential verification ledgers will be defined in alignment with applicable Bangladesh statutory requirements upon legal approval.

06 Learner Rights & Contact

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Procedures for requesting record corrections (such as certificate spelling updates) or data queries will be routed through our Banani campus operations desk via the Contact Page.
