Institutional Integrity

Academic Governance & Advisory Council Mandate

The ECDL Academic Advisory Council convenes quarterly to audit courseware validity against newly published National Board of Revenue (NBR) statutory regulatory orders (SROs), Ministry of Labour and Employment gazettes, and international corporate governance best practices.

Statutory Alignment Audits

All compliance and taxation curricula are redlined within 72 hours of any official statutory gazette notification.

Faculty Accreditation

Facilitators must undergo formal peer auditions and maintain minimum 90%+ participant satisfaction scores to retain accreditation.

Learner Feedback Audits

Direct, unedited participant evaluation forms are reviewed directly by the Academic Director to eliminate blind spots.
