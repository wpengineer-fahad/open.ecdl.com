About Enroute Center for Development Ltd.

Pioneering Corporate Learning & Executive Capability in Bangladesh

Enroute Center for Development Ltd. (ECDL) is Bangladesh’s premier executive education, statutory compliance, and corporate capability development institute. Established under the Enroute Group, we empower professionals, business leaders, and enterprise organizations to master regulatory complexity, elevate operational productivity, and build enduring leadership resilience.

Our Evolution

A Decade of Elevating Professional Capabilities

From a specialized executive training unit to Bangladesh’s premier multi-track corporate development ecosystem.

2017
Foundation

Establishment of ECDL

Founded as the specialized corporate training arm under Enroute International Limited, addressing acute capability deficits in statutory labour compliance and executive HR governance in Dhaka.

2019
Milestone

Surpassing 10,000 Professionals

Formally introduced structured masterclasses across Bangladesh Labour Act 2006, Corporate Tax, and Commercial Negotiations. Exceeded 10,000 certified corporate participants across 60+ partner enterprises.

2021
Resilience

Hybrid Studio & LMS Integration

Pioneered synchronous live-interactive cohorts during global supply chain disruptions. Integrated with Sudoksho e-learning ecosystem to deliver hybrid corporate academies for remote executive teams.

2023
Expansion

1,000+ Corporate Programs Completed

Designed customized multi-month in-house academies for leading private commercial banks PLC, multinational FMCG giants, and tier-1 export garment conglomerates. Introduced the 6-step diagnostic TNA tool.

2025
Leadership

Executive C-Suite & ESG Tracks

Launched executive masterclasses in AML/CFT compliance scoring, Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, and Workplace Wellbeing, addressing emerging international buyer standards and national tax reform.

2026
Next Horizon

Open ECDL Digital Ecosystem

Unveiled the unified Open ECDL platform: 1,500+ programs conducted, 43,000+ professionals upskilled, 200+ faculty, and full digital learning pathway certification for corporate Bangladesh.

Our Operating DNA

Five Core Institutional Commitments

The non-negotiable principles that govern how ECDL selects faculty, authors curricula, and evaluates learner outcomes.

1

Practical Relevance

Direct shopfloor and boardroom applicability takes precedence over theoretical abstraction. Every concept taught must solve a real commercial or statutory dilemma tomorrow.

2

Statutory Integrity

Uncompromising adherence to the laws and regulations of Bangladesh. We empower enterprises to achieve sustainable compliance rather than temporary legal shortcuts.

3

Pedagogical Rigour

Instructional materials are continuously peer-reviewed and vetted against current gazettes, SRO amendments, and international management standards.

4

Measurable Capability Impact

We evaluate our programs based on participant competency progression, post-course manager appraisals, and tangible client ROI, never merely course attendance.

5

Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)

Mandatory post-cohort participant feedback loops and faculty debriefs drive iterative curriculum updates every single quarter.
