Faculty Standards

What We Look for in an Open ECDL Facilitator

We do not engage theoretical lecturers. Our learners are working professionals, department managers, and senior directors who demand practical, tested operational solutions.

Statutory & Academic Verification:

Every applicant undergoes background vetting by the ECDL Academic Council. We do not accept unverified designations or honorary credentials.

01

10+ Years Field Leadership

Demonstrated career track record leading teams, resolving industrial challenges, or practicing before legal and tax tribunals.

02

Case-Based Pedagogy

Ability to structure learning around real operational dilemmas, role-plays, financial models, and actionable checklists.

03

Executive Presence

High emotional intelligence and confident vocal gravitas capable of steering lively debates with senior corporate executives.

04

Continuous Learning Mindset

Commitment to constantly updating case materials to reflect the latest Bangladesh statutory gazettes, tax circulars, and market trends.
