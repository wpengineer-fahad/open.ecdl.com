Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common procedural, tax compliance, and venue questions.

Does ECDL issue statutory Mushak 6.3 Tax Invoices for corporate payments?
Yes. Enroute Center for Development Ltd. is fully compliant under the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act 2012. We issue an original signed Mushak 6.3 Tax Invoice for all corporate training invoices, enabling your enterprise to accurately claim input tax credits or reconcile withholding VAT (VDS) obligations.
What are the accepted corporate payment methods?
We accept payments via Account Payee Corporate Cheque, Bank Electronic Fund Transfer Network (BEFTN), Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS), and Corporate Debit/Credit cards. Official receipts and treasury challan reconciliation support are provided upon transaction confirmation.
Can ECDL conduct customized in-house training at our corporate premises?
Absolutely. Over 60% of our enterprise programs are conducted directly on-site at client headquarters, factory auditoriums, or off-site executive retreats anywhere across Bangladesh. Our faculty travels nationwide with complete audio-visual and physical workshop toolkits.
What facilities are available at the Banani Executive Learning Center?
Our Banani campus features tiered executive amphitheaters, syndicate discussion pods for group breakout workshops, high-speed fiber Wi-Fi, 4K digital projectors, studio-grade hybrid broadcasting cameras, and executive tea/lunch dining lounges.
