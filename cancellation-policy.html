Cancellation, Deferral & Substitution Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 (Structural Draft) • Open Enrolment, Group Rosters & Events (Pending Approval)

Draft Notice — Pending Legal Counsel Review

This page demonstrates the structured layout for cancellation, substitution, and postponement policies. All deadlines and commercial parameters remain unapproved drafts pending review by ECDL leadership and legal counsel.

Table of Contents

1. Participant-Initiated Cancellation 2. Delegate Substitution 3. Cohort Transfer & Deferral 4. Non-Attendance (No-Show) 5. Institutional Postponement

01 Participant-Initiated Cancellation

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Written cancellation requests must be communicated via the registered email address or corporate coordinator portal prior to cohort start dates. Specific notice windows will be inserted upon legal review.

02 Delegate Substitution

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Corporate clients and group sponsors may request substitute delegate nominations (replacing an unavailable employee with another team member) prior to certificate preparation, subject to eligibility verification.

03 Cohort Transfer & Deferral

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Draft deferral guidelines outline how confirmed learners facing urgent travel or operational emergencies may request re-allocation to the next scheduled cohort of the same programme.

04 Non-Attendance (No-Show)

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Provisions governing unnotified absence on cohort kickoff dates, reserved catering/coursepack allocation, and future re-enrollment eligibility will be confirmed by legal counsel.

05 Institutional Postponement & Force Majeure

[Draft Pending Legal Review] If weather, civic disruptions, or trainer emergencies necessitate shifting an in-person Banani hall session to Live Online Zoom or a revised date, all registered learners receive priority notification and zero-fee rescheduling or refund eligibility per our draft Refund Policy.
