Case Engagement

Plant Leadership Transformation for 120 Senior Factory Supervisors

The Operational Challenge

Rapid export expansion created communication friction between shift managers, line supervisors, and central planning. Mid-level floor supervisors lacked structured people-management skills, resulting in higher overtime costs, production bottlenecks, and a 34% annual turnover among line leaders.

ECDL Consultative Solution

ECDL conducted a comprehensive on-site Training Needs Assessment (TNA), observing shift handovers and interviewing plant managers. We then co-designed a customized 8-week "Frontline Leadership Academy" featuring modular weekend workshops and bi-weekly shopfloor coaching audits.

Cohort & Execution Cadence

Delivered in 4 phased cohorts for 120 plant supervisors. Core training modules addressed root-cause problem solving, constructive worker communication, Bangladesh Labour Act compliance, and daily 10-minute lean standup meetings.

Audited Transformation Metrics

28% Reduction in supervisor turnover within 6 months
14.2% Improvement in on-time shift delivery velocity
120 Certified plant leaders with ECDL credentials
Zero Labor disputes or statutory compliance grievances
