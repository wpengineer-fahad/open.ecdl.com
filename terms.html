01 Scope & Applicability

[Draft Pending Legal Review] This section defines the scope of access to the Open ECDL web platform, online learning management interfaces, corporate client portals, and in-person executive workshops operated by Enroute Center for Development Ltd. (ECDL).

Final published terms will specify applicability across individual learners, corporate account administrators, nominated employees, and visiting faculty, distinguishing open-enrollment cohorts from custom B2B enterprise training agreements.

02 Enrolment & Cohort Eligibility

[Draft Pending Legal Review] This clause outlines how open-enrollment seat reservations, corporate group nominations, and waitlist allocations are confirmed.

  • Seat confirmation rules upon verified fee settlement or approved corporate purchase order.
  • Waitlist priority order and cohort transfer eligibility criteria.
  • Prerequisite professional background disclosures where specified in the programme prospectus.

03 Fees, Taxes & Payment Terms

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Programme fees on Open ECDL are denominated in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT). Applicable statutory VAT and Mushak Form 6.3 tax documentation rules will be formalized in the approved commercial terms.

Where offline bank transfers or corporate invoicing are permitted, this section will specify invoice settlement windows and verification procedures prior to cohort admission. For operational refund and cancellation workflows, please review our draft Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.

04 Learner Conduct & Portal Access

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Authorised account credentials for the Learner Portal and Corporate Client Portal are strictly assigned to named individuals or authorised corporate representatives.

Approved guidelines will cover respectful classroom participation, academic integrity during assessments, non-disruption of live virtual sessions, and account credential security.

05 Courseware & Intellectual Property

[Draft Pending Legal Review] All curriculum frameworks, slide decks, statutory case studies, assessment instruments, and recorded video sessions remain the intellectual property of ECDL or its respective faculty authors and licensing partners.

Draft provisions clarify that enrolled learners are granted limited personal reference access and may not commercially redistribute, resell, or publicly broadcast proprietary ECDL training packs without written authorisation.

06 Assessments & Certification

[Draft Pending Legal Review] Issuance of completion certificates is subject to satisfying programme-specific attendance, participation, and evaluation requirements established for each cohort.

Issued credentials may be verified publicly through the Certificate Verification Portal using privacy-safe verification records. Conditions governing administrative revocation will be defined upon legal sign-off.
